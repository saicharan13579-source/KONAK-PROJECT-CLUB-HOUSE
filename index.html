01 · The idea

Most clubhouses are a list of amenities. Konak's is a landscape you walk into: light falls through the middle of the building, gardens grow inside it, and every room has its own mood.

The design follows one idea, the unfolding landscape. Like a walk through a valley, the clubhouse reveals itself slowly: a shaded entrance, then a burst of greenery and daylight, then quieter rooms for play, fitness, celebration and rest. It is designed to feel like an extension of your home, not a building you visit.

02 · The courtyard
i.

A forest at the centre of the clubhouse

Where most clubhouses put a reception desk, Konak puts a garden. Step through the entrance glass and the building opens around a landscaped courtyard, lit from above, with a red ribbon sculpture floating through the full height of the space.

ii.

An indoor lawn children can run on

Soft lawn, smooth white boulders to climb and sit on, and pockets of ferns and palms. A playful indoor forest that is safe in every season and in sight of the lounges where parents sit.

iii.

The sound of water, the texture of stone

A shallow waterfall runs down a travertine feature wall. Built-in seating follows the edge of the landscape, so there is always a place to pause, read or simply watch the light change.

iv.

Everything opens onto it

Lounges, the staircase and the first-floor lobby all look into the courtyard through a large cut-out. It is the clubhouse's living room, the place every visit begins and ends.

07 · Materials

Five materials,
chosen to age well

A calm, natural palette runs through all three floors. It gives the clubhouse its quiet richness and keeps it looking timeless for years, not just on opening day.

Stone

Travertine and marble-look surfaces for the walls and floors of the main spaces.

Plaster

Soft curved ceilings and walls that guide you through the lounges.

Wood

Warm timber slat ceilings, panelling and a lounger deck by the pool.

Metal

Brass and bronze details that catch the light in the banquet hall and lobbies.

Greenery

Living plants on every floor, from the courtyard to the planter islands.
